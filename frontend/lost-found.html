How It Works

1

Post Your Item

Describe the item clearly with unique identifying marks, the last known location, and your contact method.

2

Community Helps

Others on campus can comment, share, or DM you if they spot the item.

3

Verify & Handover

Owner provides a description to confirm. Meet in a public campus area to hand it over.

4

Mark Resolved

Once returned, mark your post as resolved to let others know it has been handled.

Safety Tips

  • Always meet in public campus areas
  • Include unique identifying marks
  • Never share sensitive personal info publicly
  • Mark resolved once item is returned
